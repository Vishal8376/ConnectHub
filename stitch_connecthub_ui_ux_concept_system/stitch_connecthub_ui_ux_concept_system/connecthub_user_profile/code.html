Published in Tangible Computing Guild

Why glass flat-panels make us forget: Lessons from 14 days of ceramic haptic prototypes.

When our hands touch raw grogged clay versus glazed feldspar, our mechanoreceptors fire in distinct temporal bursts. Over the last fortnight in the studio, we measured electrical skin response while participants navigated complex spatial timelines. The takeaway was startling: tactile grain density created twice the recall permanence compared to smooth capacitive haptics.
