## Brand & Style

This design system is built around warmth, presence, and intentional human interaction. It stands in direct contrast to algorithmic, retention-driven social platforms by trading hyper-stimulating gradients, artificial neon accents, and metric obsession for tactile, editorial calm. 

The aesthetic blends **warm tactile minimalism** with **modern editorial craft**:
- **Atmosphere:** Grounded, domestic, and sunlit. Felt like natural linen, smooth clay, and warm paper stock.
- **Visual Stance:** Zero generic frosted glass or cold tech-blue/purple gradients. Structure is conveyed through warm paper tiers, subtle hairline boundaries, and organic, comfortable radii.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Intimate, unhurried, trustworthy, and communal. The interface recedes to celebrate genuine interpersonal discourse and visual stories rather than vanity metrics.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in earth minerals and natural textiles, avoiding synthetic primaries:

- **Primary Clay / Terracotta (`#E05A47`):** Represents warmth, direct action, and creative expression. Used for focal call-to-actions, vital interaction triggers, and active social states.
- **Secondary Olive Sage (`#4A7C59`):** Represents growth, mutual trust, and verification. Used for connection states, mutual friend indicators, verified community spaces, and success markers.
- **Tertiary Ochre (`#D99B4B`):** A soft, secondary warm note used sparingly for contextual highlights, bookmarks, and milestone celebrations.
- **Warm Ink Neutrals:**
  - **Ink Primary (`#1F2421`):** Deep warm charcoal for headings and high-contrast body text.
  - **Ink Muted (`#4B534D`):** Mid-tone warm slate for subtitles, secondary text, and timestamps.
  - **Ink Subtle (`#7E8881`):** Neutral stone for placeholders, inactive iconography, and quiet labels.
- **Warm Cream & Alabaster Surfaces:**
  - **Canvas Base (`#FDFBF7`):** Primary window background mimicking raw, sun-washed paper.
  - **Surface Elevated (`#F7F4EE`):** Card and post surfaces, slightly cooler and elevated from base.
  - **Surface Recessed / Input (`#EFECE6`):** Field interiors, reply wells, and conversational bubbles.
  - **Hairline Border (`#E7E3DC`):** Delicate boundary color applied to cards, dividers, and avatars.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes legibility, warm cadence, and effortless long-form reading:
- **Primary Workhorse:** `plusJakartaSans` handles navigation, feed interaction, UI controls, and conversation threads. Its humanist proportions and open apertures feel welcoming rather than mechanical.
- **Editorial Voice:** `newsreader` is reserved for intentional long-form stories, personal reflections, and community intros. This evokes printed matter, inviting readers to slow down.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** 
  - Never use pure black (`#000000`); rely solely on `Ink Primary` (`#1F2421`) to maintain print-like softness.
  - Headings sit at tight letter spacing to project deliberate intention.
  - Body copy favors a relaxed line height (`1.6` to `1.65`) for prolonged, fatigue-free reading.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a human-scale, centralized rhythm designed to eliminate cognitive overload:

- **Feed Column (`42rem` / `672px` max):** The central conversation feed never expands beyond comfortable conversational width, preventing long horizontal eye travel.
- **Responsive Model:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Asymmetrical 3-column frame. Left column (240px) hosts intentional navigation; center column (672px) contains stories and updates; right column (320px) houses active connections, community gatherings, and quiet presence indicators.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Left icon rail (72px) with a centered main feed and collapsible side drawer.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single column edge-to-edge flow with `1rem` horizontal safe inset, standard top bar, and bottom contextual navigation.
- **Breathing Rhythm:** Generous vertical spacing (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) isolates each post into an independent thought, disallowing chaotic feed blending.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects synthetic dropshadows, intense blurs, and floating cards:

- **Tonal Layering:** Hierarchy is communicated by placing surfaces over one another. Base screen starts at `#FDFBF7`. Cards and feed entries rise naturally onto `#F7F4EE`. Nested replies and composer wells sink into `#EFECE6`.
- **Delicate Hairlines:** Cards, floating navigation, and elevated panels are framed with a single crisp 1px hairline border (`#E7E3DC`).
- **Warm Ambient Occlusion:** When dynamic elevation is necessary (such as active modals, floating composer bars, or contextual popovers), depth is achieved through an ultra-soft, warm umber-tinted shadow:
  - *Floating Elevation:* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.03)`
  - *Modal Elevation:* `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04)`

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on soft, organic curvature reflecting handcrafted ceramics and paper stationery:

- **Container Surfaces (Cards, Modals):** Scaled consistently between `16px` and `20px` (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) to feel approachable and tactile without appearing toy-like.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Chips):** Use `12px` to `14px` border radius, creating comfortable touch targets that preserve structural cohesion.
- **Conversational Bubbles:** Asymmetric rounding (e.g., `18px 18px 4px 18px` for received items) to clearly signify authorship and speech direction.
- **Avatars:** Strictly circular (`rounded-full`) to complement the natural geometry of human faces.

## Components

### Buttons & Relationship Actions
- **Primary Action (Clay):** `#E05A47` background, white text, 12px radius, subtle hover deepening to `#D04B38`. No harsh drop shadow.
- **Relationship States:**
  - *Connect:* Primary Clay outline (`1.5px solid #E05A47`) with Clay text, transitioning to solid fill on press.
  - *Pending:* Soft Ochre wash (`#FDF5EB`) with `#B87B2E` text and soft dot indicator.
  - *Connected:* Subtle Sage wash (`#EDF4EF`) with `#4A7C59` text and small leaf/check indicator.
  - *Accept / Decline Pairing:* Dual button cluster with solid Sage `#4A7C59` (Accept) and hairline Alabaster outline `#4B534D` (Decline).

### Avatars & Presence Indicators
- Circular frames with a 2px `#FDFBF7` safety ring separating the image from surrounding content.
- Status indicators sit bottom-right:
  - *Active / Present:* 10px `#4A7C59` solid circle.
  - *Quiet / In Community:* 10px warm terracotta dot with cream center ring.

### Cards & Post Units
- Background `#F7F4EE` with a 1px `#E7E3DC` solid border and 16px radius.
- Padding: 20px desktop, 16px mobile.
- Clean header featuring avatar, author name in `headline-sm`, relationship proximity badge, and timestamp in `Ink Muted`.
- Interaction tray at card footer features calm, text-labeled actions (Reflect, Reply, Share Privately) rather than aggressive vanity counters.

### Conversational Bubbles & Threading
- Nested replies step inward by 20px, anchored by a vertical 1.5px hairline guide `#E7E3DC`.
- Sender bubble: `#EFECE6` with `16px` radius.
- Self bubble: Light terracotta wash (`#FAF0EE`) with deep terracotta text.

### Input Fields & Composers
- Inactive state: `#EFECE6` background with `1px solid transparent` and 12px radius.
- Active focus state: `#FDFBF7` surface with a crisp `1.5px solid #E05A47` halo and zero artificial glow.
- Human-scale post composer features a warm prompt ("What is on your mind today?"), an uncrowded expansion drawer, and media attachments enclosed in soft rounded frames.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact 10px-radius tags with `#EFECE6` fill and `#4B534D` text.
- Active filter: `#4A7C59` fill with `#FFFFFF` text for community groups, or `#E05A47` for personal topics.
