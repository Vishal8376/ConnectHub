We spent the morning testing bioswale water retention curves on the Southeast 12th Avenue redevelopment. Rather than burying stormwater runoff in subterranean conduits, our intent is to invite urban dwellers to witness rainfall as living landscape infrastructure.

Notice how the permeable basalt pavers slow the velocity just enough for native sedge roots to naturally filter micro-sediments before reaching the aquifer. Honest textures in civil engineering keep citizens connected to regional hydrology.
